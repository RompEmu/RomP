These shaders come from RetroArch's slang shader collection,
https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders at commit 0b3ff0b, and keep their own licenses:
crt-guest-advanced by guest(r) and the Game Boy shaders by Harlequin and Matt Akins
are GPL-2.0 or later, lcd-grid-v2 by cgwg is GPL, and sharp-shimmerless by zadpos
and the handheld colour shaders by hunterk and Pokefan531 are public domain.
mdapt by Sp00kyFox and the colour lookup passes state no license of their own and
are distributed here as part of that collection.

Only the files the presets Romp offers need are copied here, unchanged.
